# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of identity, background, experience, or ability.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, constructive, and considerate. Accept feedback gracefully,
focus criticism on ideas and code, and respect differing viewpoints.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexualized attention, personal
attacks, and publishing another person's private information are unacceptable.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behavior privately to the repository owner through the
contact method in [docs/SECURITY.md](docs/SECURITY.md). Maintainers may remove,
edit, or reject contributions and may temporarily or permanently ban people
whose behavior is inappropriate. Reports will be reviewed promptly and handled
as confidentially as practical.
